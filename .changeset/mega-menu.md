New `MegaMenu` with `MegaMenuCategory`, `MegaMenuGroup` and `MegaMenuLink`: an Amazon-style store catalog. A column of categories sits on one side, and the hovered category's showcase of link columns opens next to it. It is navigation built on the WAI-ARIA disclosure pattern, not a command menu: the column is a single tab stop with arrow keys, and Enter or the arrow toward the showcase moves focus into it.
