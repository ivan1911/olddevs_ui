`RichTextEditor` gets the `youtube` and `twitch` Tools: video in the Document as a link, with the player drawn by the kit (there was no requesting screen at release, ADR-0048, so the API follows the Youtube and Twitch extensions from Tiptap, which the kit does not install: link recognition and the player address are its own code).

- **`youtube` and `twitch` are in no Preset, neither `full` nor `basic`: nothing changes for existing screens, and a screen switches them on with `tools={[...RICH_TEXT_PRESETS.full, "youtube", "twitch"]}` (either one alone is fine).** Nothing new to install. The page that shows a player needs a `frame-src` line in its Content-Security-Policy: `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com https://player.twitch.tv https://clips.twitch.tv` (the hosts of the services in use; the RichTextEditor page has it).
- The Document holds a link, not a player: one shared `video` Block, `div[data-block="video"]` with `data-provider` (`youtube` or `twitch`), `data-src` (the canonical `https` link to the video) and an ordinary link inside. The player address is built at display time: YouTube always through `youtube-nocookie.com/embed/…`, Twitch with `parent` taken from the domain of the current page, so one saved Document works in an admin screen and on a public site.
- Before a click the Editor and `RichTextView` show a kit placeholder with the service name and a "Play" button, and not a single request goes to the service; after it comes an `iframe` with a `title`, `loading="lazy"`, `allow` and `referrerpolicy`. Without JavaScript the reader sees the link. In `RichTextView` the player is a lazy Island that is mounted only if the HTML has a video Block.
- One "Video" item in the Insert menu takes a link of any enabled service (an error in the field for a foreign or disabled one), and a bare video link pasted into an empty paragraph, or typed there and confirmed with `Enter`, becomes the Block. The HTML of the Tiptap extensions (`div[data-youtube-video] iframe`, `div[data-twitch-video] iframe`) is turned into the same Block, with the player address translated back into the link.
- The sanitizer is not loosened: `iframe` is still always stripped. `sanitizeRichText` lets through `div[data-block="video"]`, `data-provider` from the list and `data-src` only as the canonical `https` link to a video on the service's hosts (`www.youtube.com`, `www.twitch.tv`, `clips.twitch.tv`), and a Block with anything else loses its wrapper and stays its link. Without the Tool a video Block in `value` is shown as its link, never dropped. In Markdown a video is its link on a line of its own, and `setMarkdown` reads such a line back as a Block when the service is on.
- New public bits: the `VideoIcon` icon, and the `editor.video`, `editor.videoLink`, `editor.videoInsert`, `editor.videoNotFound`, `editor.videoPlay`, `editor.videoPlayLabel` and `editor.videoPlayer` messages.
