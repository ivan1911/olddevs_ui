Editor, extension: footnotes. The new `footnotes` Tool, in `full` (not in `basic`), puts a reference with an automatic number into the text and collects the footnotes' texts in a list at the end of the Document. The "Footnote" item of the Slash menu and of the "…" menu inserts a reference at the caret, makes its footnote and moves the caret into it. The kit keeps the list: a footnote exists while its reference does, deleting the reference deletes the footnote in the same edit (`Ctrl/⌘+Z` brings back both), and moving text renumbers. A click or `Enter` on a reference goes to its footnote, the footnote's number button or `Enter` in it goes back, and in `RichTextView` the same jumps are plain anchors that need no JavaScript. A footnote is one paragraph of text with Marks and links. Copying a reference carries its footnote's text, and a paste makes a new footnote. The Table of contents does not show a reference's number, and `getText()` and `maxLength` see `[1]`. Markdown is GFM (`[^1]` and `[^1]: text`), losing no text in either direction. The state is the footnotes in order with the current one, and the commands are `insert()`, `goToFootnote(n)` and `goToReference(n)`.

The HTML is `sup[data-footnote-ref]` with an anchor and `section[data-block="footnotes"]` with an `ol`. These are the first `id` and the first `href="#…"` in the Schema: `sanitizeRichText` keeps them only as `fn-<key>` and `fnref-<key>`, in the footnote markup, and the `link` Tool still takes no `#`. A server that sanitizes with its own allowlist built from `RICH_TEXT_SCHEMA` should take the new `section` tag and the `id`, `role`, `aria-label` and `data-footnote-ref` attributes from it. The visible change in `full`: the Slash menu and the "…" menu gain a "Footnote" item; a screen that does not want it removes the Tool from `tools` (`RICH_TEXT_PRESETS.full` without `"footnotes"`). The Tool is a lazy built-in: its nodes, keys and Markdown rules arrive as a chunk through the Tool's Loader, only to an Editor that has `footnotes` in `tools`, and the Skeleton draws the references and the list from `value` until it arrives.
