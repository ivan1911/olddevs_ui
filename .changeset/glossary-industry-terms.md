The English Reference now calls borrowed concepts by the words of their origin, so they can be found by search: the Bubble menu (was Selection menu) and the Slash menu (was Insert menu), as in Tiptap; the table toolbar, menu aim, color scale, compound component, filtered rows, menu button, keyboard shortcuts dialog, Keyboard shortcuts popover, Source mode, User edit, Programmatic replacement, Status bar, Column layout and Callout tone. Only the text changes: props, values and exports stay the same.

The glossary gets two columns, "Source" (where the English word comes from) and "Also searched as" (synonyms used elsewhere), a `chip` row, and BubbleMenu, FloatingMenu and Suggestion rows in its Tiptap section: `toolbar="floating"` is the floating Toolbar above a selection, built on Tiptap's BubbleMenu, not Tiptap's FloatingMenu.
